Time semantics are separated. observedAt means the source says the phenomenon or position was observed at that time. publishedAt means the source issued or published the record. validAt means an alert, model, or forecast is valid for that time. fetchedAt is only when this browser retrieved the source. Fetch time never substitutes for event age, forecast validity, or publication age.

On this page, NHC browser ingestion places official storm centers from CurrentStorms.json. Official NHC forecast-track and cone geometry is ingested through the broker/Earth path when that broker is configured; this page does not pretend those KMZ products are rendered here.

FORECAST / REASONING BOUNDARY

Month/year only when the mathematics actually qualifies it

D.O.M. may calculate statistical threshold windows from sufficiently long, quality-weighted time series and authoritative scenario ensembles. It must refuse a month/year result when trend fit, uncertainty, sample count, temporal span or physical model support is inadequate. Tornadoes, earthquakes and other chaotic hazards are never assigned deterministic future dates merely because a regression line exists.
