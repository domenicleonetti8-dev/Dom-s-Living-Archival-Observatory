REFERENCES & SOURCE INDEX

Primary systems cited by D.O.M.

Individual search results and hazard records retain their own direct source links when available. This index identifies principal archives, scholarly registries, government systems and observing networks referenced or targeted by the public interface; inclusion here does not itself mean a live adapter is active.

  1. Project Gutenberg — public-domain books and bibliographic metadata.
  2. Library of Congress — archival, government and cultural collections.
  3. Internet Archive — archived books, media and web collections.
  4. Crossref — DOI and scholarly publication metadata.
  5. OpenAlex — scholarly works, authors, institutions and citation metadata.
  6. PubMed — biomedical and life-science literature.
  7. Data.gov — United States government datasets.
  8. ClinicalTrials.gov — registered clinical studies.
  9. NASA Earthdata — Earth-observation data and imagery systems.
  10. Zenodo and DataCite — research datasets, records and persistent identifiers.
  11. Europe PMC — life-science literature and research records.
  12. USGS Earthquake Hazards Program — earthquake observations and event feeds.
  13. NASA EONET — open natural-event aggregation.
  14. NOAA / National Weather Service — official United States weather alerts and observations.
  15. NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center — operational space-weather products.
  16. Tsunami.gov / NOAA warning centers — official tsunami products and warning-center information.
